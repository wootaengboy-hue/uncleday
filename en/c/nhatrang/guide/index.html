How much should I tip?

How much should I tip?

Tipping is not mandatory in Vietnam. One guide suggests 20,000–50,000 VND if you were happy; Vietnam Tourism suggests 50,000–100,000 VND for spa therapists in the city. [1] [2]

How do I give the tip?

Hand it directly to your therapist rather than leaving it at the front desk, where it may not reach them. [2]

Is the tip sometimes included?

Yes. Some spas include the tip in the price, so check the menu or receipt for “tip included / no tip” before paying so you don’t tip twice. When you book through Uncle Day we tell you in advance.

Last stop before your flight

Cam Ranh airport is more than 30 km from downtown and many flights leave at night. A common plan is to store luggage after check-out, have a massage and shower, then head to the airport. Evenings from 7–10 pm get busy, so book 2–3 days ahead. [1]

Can I get a massage right after eating?
Wait at least an hour after a meal and avoid massages after drinking. Tell your therapist your preferred pressure before starting.
Can I walk in without a booking?
Some downtown shops take walk-ins, but 7–10 pm is busy. Book ahead for your preferred time.
