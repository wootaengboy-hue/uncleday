How much should I tip?

How much should I tip?

Tipping is not mandatory, but 50,000–100,000 VND is typical for spa therapists in the city. [1]

How do I give the tip?

Hand it directly to your therapist rather than leaving it at the front desk, where it may not reach them. [1]

Is the tip sometimes included?

Yes. Check the receipt or counter notice before paying; if it isn’t included, hand it to your therapist after the treatment. [2]

Last stop before your flight

Many flights leave at night and Tan Son Nhat airport is fairly close to the city. Luggage storage and showers vary by shop, so check when booking. Popular spas can be full on the day, so book at least a day ahead. [2]

Uncle Day shops with luggage storage, showers or drop-off: Golden Lotus Spa & Sauna, Swan Spa & Wellness

Cosmetic and dermatology clinics

Ho Chi Minh City also has many cosmetic surgery and dermatology clinics. Treatments start with a consultation; Uncle Day can arrange the consultation and help with scheduling and costs. Some clinics offer a free first consultation or airport pickup. Always discuss surgery thoroughly with the doctor before deciding.
